# Story as a puzzle dependency chart

The story is a puzzle dependency chart, the way classic adventures are built (Monkey Island, Thimbleweed Park, Gabriel Knight). It is not a chain of cutscenes that each end in three replies. The route is cut into chapters. Inside a chapter the world is open: he can look, take, use, give and talk, and any scene whose prerequisites are met can be played, in any order. Each chapter narrows to one bottleneck, usually going to bed, and that opens the next chapter. Doing the wrong thing gets a line and leaves the chapter as it was. It does not open a second path.

This replaces the route 1 pattern where every beat is a menu of three options that all lead to the same place. Those menus are what cause the combinatorial explosion. The "options" should be objects in the room.

- Size: L
- Needs:
  - code: a chart of gates (each with what it requires) and chapters (a time label, a goal and a bottleneck), checked against story state. Object actions and scene starts are locked by it, and the chapter advances only when its bottleneck gate is used. Builds on the Interact layer, the action popup, inventory and phone from adventure-mechanics.
  - checks: a test that walks the chart. Every gate is reachable, no order of play inside a chapter leads to a dead end, every bottleneck can always be reached, and every gate in the chart is used by some Yarn target. The same data can draw the chart (next to `dialogue_graph`) so it can be read.
  - dialogue: chapters rewritten as open time with a few gates, instead of `next_scene` chains. Her texts become the stated goal of the chapter. A scene that can be played first or second reads flags instead of assuming what came before.
  - map: objects that are the gates (doors, bed, drawers, shop shelves), not just talk targets. They need to be visible objects with hotspots in the 384-px rooms.
- Depends on: Pivot: adult branching story; adventure-mechanics (inventory, action popup, phone). Route 1 scenes already written (viewing through the second Thursday) would be refit, not thrown out.
- Decided (Janosch, 2026-10-26, in conversation):
  - The structure is a puzzle dependency chart. Scenes inside a chapter may be played in different orders, and that is a goal, not a side effect.
  - The unit is a chapter, not a day. A chapter has a time label ("Sunday, morning", "Thursday, eight o'clock"), and a card shows it. Usually a chapter is a whole day, and sometimes it is half of one, so a day with two goals is two chapters. He does not choose to advance time.
  - A gate is a condition, not a choice. Her door on Thursday starts dinner because it is Thursday, it is time, and he is holding what she named. Wrong bottle: she sends him back out. Same door, different line, same chapter.
  - One open goal per chapter, said in her voice on the phone (the quest log). A goal can have several parts that he does in any order (the wine and the socks). One new location per chapter at most. A place with nothing to do yet does not open.
  - Wrong actions never fail the run and never fork the timeline. A flag may remember how he did it, for one callback later. A chapter with two real outcomes is rare and is its own chapter. The crisis beat can be one. An ordinary Thursday is not.
  - Going to bed is the usual bottleneck, once everything else is done. Before that, the bed says it is too early.
  - The chart lives in Yarn: chart nodes that are never run, plus `$chapter` and `$gate_*` variables (2026-10-26, OpenSpec change `story-chart`).
  - No skip. A stuck chapter is his to solve, and the phone note says what is left.
  - All of route 1 is refit to chapters before the next scene is written.
  - Inside a scene she is leading, talk does not advance it. His inputs are the object actions she asked for (fetch, wear, stand, wait). The scene plays when the last one is done, with no menu inside it.
- Route 1 as a first chart (as the scenes are wired today, with the bed bottlenecks added):

  ```mermaid
  flowchart TD
    subgraph C0["Chapter: the viewing"]
      knock3b[Knock on 3B] --> viewing[Viewing scene]
    end
    viewing --> movein[Move-in, scripted<br/>gets blush socks]
    subgraph C1["Chapter: Sunday, morning"]
      text1[Her text: bring a croissant] --> knock3a1
      bag[Bakery bag, given] --> knock3a1[Knock on 3A]
      knock3a1 --> croissant[Croissant scene]
      croissant --> bed1[Bed]
    end
    movein --> text1
    subgraph C2["Chapter: Thursday, eight o'clock"]
      knock3a2[Knock on 3A] --> sit1[Sit at her table] --> dinner[Dinner scene]
      dinner --> sink[Wash up] --> dishes[Dishes scene] --> bed2[Bed]
    end
    bed1 --> knock3a2
    subgraph C3["Chapter: Thursday, a week later"]
      text2[Her text: bring what she'd want]
      text2 --> clue[Recycling: the bottle she drinks]
      clue -.-> wine
      text2 --> wine[Buy wine downstairs]
      text2 --> socks[Find and put on the blush socks]
      text2 -.-> gifts[Tulips / chocolate, optional]
      wine --> knock3a3[Knock on 3A]
      socks -.-> knock3a3
      gifts -.-> knock3a3
      knock3a3 --> give[Give, shoes off]
      give --> cork[Corkscrew from her sink] --> open[Open wine at her table]
      open --> sit2[Sit] --> dinner2[Second dinner scene]
      dinner2 --> phone[Her texts that night] --> bed3[Bed]
    end
    bed2 --> text2
    bed3 --> next[Card: Thursdays.]
  ```

  Solid arrows are required, dashed ones are optional or change only her line. What the chart shows about today's scenes:
  - The second Thursday is already chart-shaped: wine, socks and gifts can be done in any order before her door. Most of its refit is removing the menus inside the scenes.
  - The viewing, move-in, croissant and first dinner are straight lines. Each is one gate and one scene, so their menus are the only thing the player does. These are where objects should replace the replies.
  - Today the first dinner jumps straight into the second Thursday with `next_scene`, and the croissant jumps to the Thursday card. The chart puts a bed between them, so each chapter has a real end.
  - Order can be loosened most cheaply in chapters that hold two scenes with no story order between them. In the route 1 outline, beats 6 and 7 (things at home, her things in 3B) could be one chapter whose two scenes play in either order.
