# artgen: sprite sheet frames into the game sheet format

`artgen.py sheet` draws one direction (side view, facing right) as N frames in one image. The game sheet needs 10 columns per clip and down/left/right/up rows (960x2304, 96x192 cells, see the realistic-character-sprites sprite-format spec). `build_sheets.py` assembles sheets from separate key frames, not from `sheet` output.

- Size: M
- Needs:
  - tooling: map sheet frames onto clip columns (5 drawn frames -> 10 columns?), one sheet per direction or mirroring for left/right, front/back walk cycles in `pose.py` (only `walk_side` exists).
  - art: decide frame counts and whether mirrored left/right is acceptable.
- Depends on: artgen-qwen-image change (done).
