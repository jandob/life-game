## Leverage ladder

1. **Manners**: rules, small favours, being on time. (Already played.)
2. **Approval**: praise and touch as rewards ("That's for doing as you're told").
3. **Intimacy**: she sets the pace in bed, and he wants more of it.
4. **Knowing him**: her notes say things about him he hasn't admitted yet.
5. **The flat**: her father, the lease, the spare key. Where he lives depends on staying on her good side.

The domination thread below runs alongside the ladder: each rung she gains outside the bedroom shows up inside it too.

## Domination thread (sexual)

She is dominant in bed from the start, and it escalates in the same slow steps as everything else. It is consensual throughout. She asks about his limits in beat 3 and gives him a safeword, which also gives her something to test against later.

1. **Waiting.** She sets the pace and he is told to wait, to hold still, and not to touch until she says so. (Beat 2.)
2. **Permission.** Orgasm control: he finishes when she allows it, and asking becomes part of it. Her praise ("good") turns from a manners reward into a sexual one. (Beats 3 and 4.)
3. **Restraint and positions.** Hands tied or held, kneeling, being used for her pleasure first. She plays with his receptive side (beat 3's limits talk pays off here). (Beats 3 to 5.)
4. **Rules carry into the bedroom.** Punishment and reward, kept to her style: dry and observant, never cruel. Breaking a daytime rule has a nighttime consequence. (Beats 5 and 6.)
5. **Her terms.** Denial over days (chastity is an option if Janosch wants it), and she decides when he's allowed to touch himself at all. This ties the sex to the feminine stage: "good girl" first appears here, as a test of how he reacts. (Beats 6 to 8.)
6. **Beyond hetero (Janosch, 2026-10-20).** He starts out straight, and so is she. Being with a man is not about her desire. It is the far end of the feminine side she leads him into: if he is becoming her girl, she decides what that includes. It moves in small steps, always talked through first:
   - **Her questions.** Fantasy talk in bed and lines in her notebook ("Looked at the waiter twice.", "Asked what it feels like for me."). She frames his feminine side as wanting to be wanted the way she is. This is low-key and comes early (beat 3 or 4).
   - **Her toys.** Pegging is framed as her being the man for a night. It ties the receptive side to gender roles.
   - **Being looked at.** Once he is dressed (beats 6 to 8), she points out men noticing him, and asks how it felt.
   - **A man.** Under her direction, a man from her circle (an adult friend, introduced in beat 4) is with him while she leads the scene. This is the highest step, and it sits at the crisis around beat 8 or in the ending. Whether it happens depends on his earlier choices.
   - Each step can be declined in a choice. Declining changes her reaction, and the route stays playable.
7. **Ownership.** By the ending he asks her for it unprompted. In ending A this is the "knows it now" moment; in ending B it is what he walks away from.

Each beat climbs one rung at most. The player can always resist in a choice, but resisting changes her reaction, not the destination (flavour, as so far), until the crisis beat.

## Beats

1. **Thursdays (a short run of weeks).** The dinner becomes a ritual. Her texts slide from "bring red wine" to "wear the socks". Their first real kiss happens on her terms, at her door. *Pull:* it is the best part of his week. *Rung 2.*
2. **The knock.** Late one night she knocks on 3B and he answers, as the rule says. It is the first time she's inside since the viewing. Things get physical, and she keeps telling him to wait. Next morning she says "You're my boyfriend now. I decided." *Rung 2 to 3.*
3. **The notebook.** He sees more of her notes, now about the night. She makes it a conversation: what he liked, what he wouldn't say out loud. This is the first scene of her leading their exploration of his receptive side, with limits talked through first (realistic, and it makes the later pushes feel earned). *Rung 3 to 4.* (Explicit.)
4. **Her world.** A night out with her friends. She introduces him as hers, orders for him, and answers questions put to him. The wanderer could appear here in passing. *Push:* small public control. *Pull:* belonging somewhere in a city where he knew no one.
5. **The landlord.** Her father comes back. There's a problem with the flat (a rent rise, a late payment, a complaint about noise after eleven). She sorts it out with one phone call, and now he owes her. It stays unclear whether she caused the problem. *Rung 5.*
6. **Nobody will see.** Appearance starts, at home only: underwear, painted toenails ("you wear socks anyway"), shaving. It moves on from the blush socks and is framed as her private game. First sprite or portrait variant (stage 1).
7. **Her things in 3B.** She starts keeping clothes in his wardrobe, then choosing his. She uses the spare key and is sometimes there when he comes home. The flat begins to feel like hers. *Push and pull meet:* he notices he likes coming home to it.
8. **Going out.** She wants him to go out with something visible (an outfit, make-up, or just the nails). This is the crisis: the first choice that really branches. He goes along with it, refuses, or turns it back on her. A fight is possible. Stage 2 art.
9. **Ending.** See below.

That's eight beats plus the ending, in line with the 6 to 10 the route 1 idea estimates. Beats 1, 4 and 7 are cheap (one location, little new art). Beats 3 and 8 cost the most (explicit scene art, new sprite stages).

## Ending direction (pick one, or keep two as the route's split)

- **A. "Knows it now."** He takes the last step himself without being told. She reads him a fourth line in her notebook. It closes the "Doesn't know it yet" thesis, and it suits "both pull and push".
- **B. Too far.** He refuses at the crisis, and she makes the flat the price. He leaves 3B: a bittersweet ending, and the only one where the lock-in goes into reverse.
- **C. Even.** He pushes back and she respects it. They end as a couple where she leads but he has a say. Softer, and less of this route's kink.

Recommendation: A as the main ending and B as the alternative at beat 8, so the route's one real branch carries weight. C fits better as flavour inside A.
